The name

Bluarc: the blue arc.

Bluarc reads as “blue arc”: the thin arc of atmosphere visible at Earth's edge. Every person and every plant shares it.

Our symbol is one gesture. An upright rises from the ground and bends into an arc over a whole disc. The arc never touches the disc. That constant gap expresses how we want to work: shelter rather than control.

The name

BluArc: the blue arc.

BluArc reads as “blue arc”: progress drawn as an arc, growing from one human root.

Our symbol is four strands that rise from one shared root, bend outward and each end in a seed. People come first; every era of work, from the humanities to industry and AI, grows from them. Each strand ends in a seed, not a flower: good work leaves something behind that carries on.

Four strands. One root.

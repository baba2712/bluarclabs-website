Why it matters

Most machines still can't tell you how they are.

Motors, pumps, fans and gearboxes often run without continuous monitoring, because wiring sensors into an existing plant is slow and expensive. Wireless sensing changes that equation.

When equipment can report its own condition, maintenance teams can see problems developing and plan their response, instead of finding out when a line stops.

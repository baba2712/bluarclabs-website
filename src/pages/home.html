Why plants are different

The plant floor is not an office.

Industrial technology has to work inside constraints that office IT was never designed for. We start from those constraints.

01

Uptime comes first

Production can't stop for an update. Changes wait for planned shutdowns, and anything that risks the line is not an option.

02

Systems last decades

Operator stations, controllers and machines often run for many years, long after mainstream support has ended.

03

Conditions are harsh

Heat, dust, vibration and electrical noise decide what hardware survives and which measurements can be trusted.

04

Teams need one picture

Operations, maintenance and IT see the plant differently. Good decisions need a shared, clear view.

Our focus

Three disciplines. One operating context.

Plants run on computers, data and hardware at the same time. We work on all three, together, so each one strengthens the others.

How we work

Specific about the context. Modest about the promise.

  1. 01

    Start on the floor

    We begin with the plant itself: the equipment, the people, the constraints and how work really happens.

  2. 02

    Agree what better means

    Before anything is installed, we agree with the plant team what a good outcome looks like and how to measure it.

  3. 03

    Start small

    One line, one asset class or one site first, inside existing maintenance windows and change control.

  4. 04

    Scale what works

    Expand only what has proven its value on the floor, with security part of the design at every step.
